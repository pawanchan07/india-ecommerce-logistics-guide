# Deep Research: How India's E-commerce Delivery & Logistics Industry Actually Works

## Research Objective

I want to deeply understand the **Indian e-commerce logistics and delivery industry**, specifically the movement of physical products ordered online from a merchant/brand/D2C website to the end consumer.

Do **not** focus primarily on food delivery, restaurant delivery, or ride-hailing.

Focus on companies and networks such as:

* Delhivery
* Blue Dart
* DTDC
* Ecom Express
* XpressBees
* Shiprocket
* Shadowfax
* Ekart
* Amazon Shipping / Amazon Transportation Services
* India Post / Speed Post where relevant
* Other important Indian logistics, shipping aggregation, courier, fulfillment, and last-mile companies you discover

The objective is NOT simply to understand what these companies do.

I want to understand **the underlying business model, operating model, economics, incentives, labor model, technology, and competitive dynamics of the industry.**

The central question is:

> **When an Indian consumer buys a product online from a merchant, how does that package actually move through the logistics network, who gets paid at every stage, who bears the risk, how are delivery workers incentivized, and why does the merchant choose one logistics partner over another?**
